# **Literature Review: AI-Based Smart Traffic Management**

Research in intelligent transportation systems has increasingly focused on using Artificial Intelligence to improve traffic management.

### **Computer Vision-Based Traffic Monitoring**

Computer vision techniques can analyze images and video captured by traffic cameras to detect and count vehicles. These techniques provide useful information about traffic density without requiring physical sensors on every road.

**Advantage:** Provides visual and real-time traffic information.

**Limitation:** Performance may be affected by poor lighting, weather conditions, or camera positioning.

### **Machine Learning for Traffic Prediction**

Machine learning models can analyze historical and real-time traffic data to identify patterns and predict future traffic conditions.

**Advantage:** Can support prediction-based traffic management.

**Limitation:** Requires sufficient and representative training data.

### **IoT-Based Traffic Monitoring**

Internet of Things devices can collect information from sensors installed on roads and vehicles. This data can be transmitted to a central system for analysis.

**Advantage:** Enables continuous data collection and monitoring.

**Limitation:** Requires communication infrastructure and maintenance.

### **Intelligent Traffic Signal Control**

AI-based signal control systems can use traffic information to adjust signal timings dynamically instead of depending only on fixed schedules.

**Advantage:** Can respond to changing traffic conditions.

**Limitation:** Implementation can be complex, particularly across large networks of intersections.

### **Research Gap**

Existing approaches often focus on individual aspects such as vehicle detection, traffic prediction, or signal optimization. A major opportunity is to develop integrated systems that combine **real-time traffic monitoring, AI-based prediction, and adaptive signal control** into a unified framework.
